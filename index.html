Questions before you order

Short answers. Expand a row for detail.

Is this a real VPS product I can buy today?

No. This page is a TechAbout hiring assessment demo with invented prices. It is not connected to live PKHosting checkout.

How does annual billing save money?

You pay for 10 months and get 12. The card shows the equivalent monthly rate — for Growth that is Rs 4,000/mo when billed annually — plus the yearly total and savings.

Can I switch currency?

Yes. Use the Currency control in the header for PKR, USD, or GBP. Rates on this demo are fixed: 1 USD = 278.50 PKR and 1 GBP = 355.00 PKR. Your choice is remembered after a reload.

Do I get root access on every plan?

Yes. All four tiers include full root access. Starter ships with a lighter control panel; Growth and above include the full panel.

Which payment methods work for Pakistani buyers?

See the footer. This demo lists JazzCash, Easypaisa, local bank transfer, Visa/Mastercard, and PayPal — chosen for local wallets plus cards for diaspora and agency billing.
