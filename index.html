PKHosting VPS FAQs

Answers about plans, billing, currency, access, and payments on this demo page.

Is this a real VPS product I can buy today?

No. This page is a TechAbout hiring assessment demo. The plans, prices, and specs are invented for review only. Nothing here is connected to live PKHosting checkout, billing, or provisioning, and you cannot place a real order from this page.

How does annual billing save money?

Annual billing charges 10 months for 12 months of service. The pricing cards show the equivalent monthly rate, the yearly total, and how much you save compared with paying month to month. For example, Growth is Rs 4,800 per month on monthly billing, and Rs 4,000 per month when billed annually (Rs 48,000 for the year).

Can I switch the displayed currency?

Yes. Use the PKR, USD, or GBP menu in the floating header. This demo uses fixed conversion rates with no live exchange API: 1 USD equals 278.50 PKR, and 1 GBP equals 355.00 PKR. PKR amounts are whole numbers; USD and GBP show two decimal places. Your currency and billing period choices are stored in the browser and survive a page reload.

Do I get root access on every plan?

Yes. Starter, Growth, Scale, and Dedicated Core all include full root access so you can install and configure the software you need. Starter includes a basic control panel. Growth, Scale, and Dedicated Core include the full control panel. Dedicated Core also adds phone support on top of ticket support.

Which payment methods would a Pakistani VPS buyer use?

The footer lists JazzCash, Easypaisa, local bank transfer, Visa/Mastercard, and PayPal. JazzCash and Easypaisa cover common mobile wallets in Pakistan. Bank transfer suits larger invoices such as Dedicated Core. Cards and PayPal help diaspora and agency clients who pay from abroad. Brand logos are not hotlinked; methods are shown as text labels only.

What happens if JavaScript is turned off?

The page still opens offline from index.html. Monthly PKR prices stay visible on the plan cards, and the full comparison table remains in the HTML. The FAQ still expands with native details elements. The monthly/annual toggle and currency menu will not update prices until JavaScript is available.
